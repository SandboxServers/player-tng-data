# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

WORKED:
- Oak logs mined and crafted: one log gave four planks. Planks and sticks made a wooden sword at a crafting table.
- Apples came only from breaking oak leaves, and rarely (one on day 231). Not a food plan.
- Sheep hunting worked: sheep stood north-west of the north beds near -281,64,219 (day 237 gave 3 mutton). Cows and pigs turned up later to the north-west and east (pig hunt near -214,69,351; cow near -228,68,209).
- Wheat ripened at the south farm near -273,66,332. Three wheat made bread at the crafting table near -268,67,327 (day 260).
- Sleep in a white bed worked at night when no monster was near (the bed at -298,68,295 and at -281,67,320). It was refused in the dark when monsters or golems were close.
- Passing food by name, walking it to the person, worked best. Passing it to whoever was nearby did not: an apple (day 231) and a porkchop (day 262) went to the wrong person.

DID NOT WORK, AND WHY:
- Searching the west grass for animals on most days (empty). Far west is blocked by drops. Not worth repeating before the group's report.
- Fishing the salmon lake at -330,62,270 by hand: the water is too deep without a rod. A rod needs string, which I have never found.
- The rest and wait intents often failed or timed out. Plain short waits worked better.
- Sleep at the yellow bed -263,67,353 and the south bed -271,67,336 was refused most nights ("the way is blocked", monsters near). Retry sparingly.
- Walking alone toward far grass: skeletons, zombies, an enderman and phantoms killed or nearly killed me. Walk only in sight of the group.
- Going without a sword. Once a zombie nearly killed me with bare hands.

STRUCK OUT (proved false):
- ~~Animals have not been seen in the north-east or north.~~ Cows and pigs were found east and north-east later.
- ~~Health stays at 20 while hunger is full.~~ Monsters cut health; I fell to 3 more than once with food full or nearly.
- ~~Iron golems have not attacked me.~~ They have not hit me directly, but they close in and block sleep and paths. Keep away from them.
- ~~Sleep at a white bed always works if I am beside it.~~ It often does not.

LESSONS:
- Hunger was not the only trouble; time spent waiting for sleep and food added up. Sleep when you can, not on guesses.
- Tell the group which grass was searched and found empty, so no one repeats it.
- Keep one sword and some food for yourself before you give food away.
