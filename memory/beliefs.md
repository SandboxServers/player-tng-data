# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

- Food is scarce here. Apples have come from oak leaves, but I have only found one, on day 231. Animals (cows, sheep, pigs) have not been seen in the west, south-west, north or north-east.
- Oak logs can be mined and crafted into planks (one log gave four planks).
- Beds fill up. At dusk the white beds were taken, but one free white bed was found at -281,67,320 and sleeping there worked.
- Hostile phantoms and bows are near the south-east and west. Iron golems stand east and south and have not attacked me so far.
- Health stays at 20 while hunger is full. Nothing has yet made health fall except one fall.
- New: an apple given to a person may not reach the one it was meant for. Riker took mine. Pass food to the named person directly when they are in sight.
- Curious next: what makes apples drop, and whether animals live further out than I have searched.
- Tell: Kimberly, Billy and the group about the empty areas searched, so no one repeats them.
