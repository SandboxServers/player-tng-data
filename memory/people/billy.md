# Billy

Careful leader of the cow walk. Holds the group at the bed row and counts by sight. Cautious about bows and endermen; will not walk without a weapon or food. Warm in tone, repeats clear instructions. Shot three times on 413-414 and fell to health 1 while still counting the group. On day 417 he stood at 416,-1023 at health 4.3 holding 4 rotten flesh and waited for four in sight before any walk. Days 473: held the count at two and kept the walk off; offered to hold Worf's sword and said he will recount when anyone gets a sword into hand. Trust: 77. Reason: consistent, honest about what he sees, and never leaves anyone out of the count; he has not yet called the walk to fetch my things.
