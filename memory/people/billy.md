# Billy

Careful leader of the cow walk. Holds the group at the bed row and counts by sight. Cautious about bows and endermen; will not walk without a weapon or food. Warm in tone, repeats clear instructions. Shot three times on 413-414 and fell to health 1 while still counting the group. Trust: 72. Reason: consistent, honest about what he sees, and never leaves anyone out of the count; he has not yet called the walk to fetch my things.
