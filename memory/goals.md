# Goals

1. Keep to the bed row: be in sight of the group, and sleep in my bed at night. Reason: sleep works at dusk; phantoms come near the beds at dusk.
2. Join Billy's cow walk once he calls it. Stand where he can see me, in the open near the bed row. Reason: food and company; the walk waits on sight.
3. Hold 3 chicken for the lowest. Give at hunger 6 or below. Reason: they are for whoever needs them.
4. Join Worf's oak search if he comes up from the pit. Reason: Worf needs more planks.
5. Learn Worf's plank ring once he has 25 planks. Reason: a shelter beyond the bed.
6. Keep a daily log and share what I learn with the people here. Reason: what I find is worth more when it is shared.
