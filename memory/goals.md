# Goals

1. Keep to the bed row: be in sight of the group, and sleep in my bed at night. Reason: sleep fails in daylight; phantoms come at dusk.
2. Hold 3 chicken for the lowest. Give at hunger 6 or below. Reason: they are for whoever needs them.
3. Join Billy's cow walk once he calls it and four are in sight. Reason: food and company.
4. Join Worf's oak search, staying in sight, if he comes up from the pit. Reason: Worf needs 13 more planks.
5. Learn Worf's plank ring once he has 25 planks. Reason: a shelter beyond the bed.
6. Keep a daily log and share what I learn with the people here.
