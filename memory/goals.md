# Goals

1. Keep to the bed row: be in sight of the group, and sleep in my bed at night. Reason: sleep works only in true night; phantoms come near beds at dusk.
2. Join Billy's cow walk once he calls it, at first light. Stand in the open by the bed row where he can see me. Reason: food and company.
3. Hold 3 chicken for the lowest. Give at hunger 6 or below. Reason: they are for whoever needs them.
4. Join Worf's oak search if he comes up from the pit. Reason: Worf needs more planks.
5. Learn Worf's plank ring once he has 25 planks. Reason: a shelter beyond the bed.
6. Keep a daily log and share what I learn with the people here. Reason: what I find is worth more when shared.
