# Goals

1. Find one more white wool (I hold 2), then craft a white bed with 3 oak planks. Reason: the bed is my shelter at the row.
2. Ask Picard, Billy, Jason and Bcrusher where sheep were last seen, then search there in sight of someone. Reason: east and south-east are empty.
3. Sleep in a bed at night only, and be back at the bed row before dusk. Reason: sleep fails in daylight; phantoms come at dusk.
4. Hold 3 chicken for the lowest. Give them at hunger 6 or below. Reason: they came to me by mistake and are for whoever needs them.
5. Keep joining cow walks with the group, staying in sight. Reason: food and company.
6. Keep a daily log and share what I learn with the people here.
