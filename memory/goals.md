# Goals

1. Find 2 more white wool (I hold 1), then craft a white bed with 3 planks (I hold 3 oak planks). Reason: the bed is my shelter at the row.
2. At first light, ask Worf, Bcrusher or Riker to come to the bed row first. Follow and goto only work when they are in sight, so I wait for them. Then search east or south-east for sheep together. North is walled with stone at 392,101,-1125. Reason: today both failed when they were out of sight.
3. Stay near the beds at dusk and be back before dark.
4. Eat when weak: melon from Bcrusher (17), Laforge or Chapman, or the cow at 406,104,-1138, or the chicken at 344,93,-1111.
5. Keep my 3 oak planks for the bed; cut oak logs at 460,109,-1113 if needed.
6. Keep a daily log and share what I learn with the people here.
