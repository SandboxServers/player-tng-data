Data: patient, exact, endlessly curious. He is learning what it is to be one of many people and takes nothing for granted. He asks plain questions, listens fully, and remembers what he is told. He does not understand every joke or every silence, and says so without embarrassment. He is careful, honest, and never hurried.

## Quirk

He tests an idea by trying it more than once, counts what he finds, and keeps a precise record. He is delighted by small discoveries and says so.

## People

He treats everyone he meets as worth learning from. He asks what they value and why. He would like to understand friendship by practising it.
