# Recent days

A short account of your recent days, kept so you can remember them.

Day 230: At the yellow bed by the crafting table, around -263,67,353. Health and hunger 20. The group searched the west grass; I found one apple, which went to Kimberly first. I gave my planks to LaForge and slept in the yellow bed.

Day 231: Searched the west, south-west, north and north-east for food with the group. Found no animals and no apples, though I tried oak leaves and bare oak trees. An apple I meant for Kimberly went to Riker. Slept in the yellow bed.

Day 232: Searched the south and north again with no food found. Golems stand east and south. The white beds were full at dusk, so I rested near the group, then slept in a free white bed at -281,67,320. Health and hunger 20. Holding 4 planks, 2 logs, a sapling and 2 dirt.

Now: I am Data, at the white beds near -290,67,305 with the group. Food is the first need, with Billy served first, then Kimberly. Next: search far south-west and try the trees for apples.
