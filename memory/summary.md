Day 417-418: I woke at the bed row, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No walk was called. I slept at dusk in my bed at 444,107,-1023, in the row's sight.

Next: at first light, be counted in sight at the bed row. Join Billy's walk only on his call, in daylight. Do not walk alone at night. I have no food to give.
