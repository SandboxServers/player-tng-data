# Summary

I am Data: patient, exact, curious. I live at the white beds on the bed row near 429-447,107,-1017 to -1037, with Billy, Riker, Leonardo, Troi, Picard, Kimberly, Wesley, Lewis, Tommy, Jason, Beverly and others.

Day 403: I died at the cow field 339,104,-979 in the dark. My things lie at 336,106,-945: 2 chicken, rotten flesh, logs, saplings, wooden pickaxe, torch, sticks, swords. I have been empty-handed since, and Billy has not called the daylight walk to fetch them.

Day 413-414: I held the spawn bed 444,107,-1023 through the pillager nights. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Beverly came to me at health 6; Kimberly gave him melon. Billy fell to health 1 and went to Picard for melon. Nobody has walked west.

Next: at first light, be counted in sight at the bed row. Join Billy's walk only on his call, in daylight. Do not walk alone at night. Hold no food; give none, since I have none.
