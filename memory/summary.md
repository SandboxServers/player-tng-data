# Summary
I am Data: patient, exact, curious. I live at the white bed row; my bed is 401,107,-1023 (I stand at 402,107,-1023 beside it). Billy leads the group; I wait for his named call.

Day 660: held at my bed with the row all day. Billy named a walk to x=380 for others, not for me, so I stayed. Health 13, hunger 17, steady. Slept at night beside my bed in the row's sight; it worked.

I hold 6 oak logs for the group (lent by Chapman and Clark), 1 plank, 2 sticks. No sword. My things lie at 336,106,-945 from day 403. Water: 466,106,-1086. Oak: 414,104,-1126. Cows seen at 339,104,-979 (Wesley's note).

Beliefs: a bed takes sleep only at night; the plain sleep beside my bed works. Wait for full night before sleeping. Clark and Deanna hold potatoes for hunger 10. Health does not regenerate while hunger is low.

Next: say holding at the row on waking. Go only when Billy names me on a walk. Ask for food only at hunger 6 or below. Curious: where sheep graze; whether the west route is clear. Tell Billy, Leonardo, Worf, Deanna.
