# Summary

I am Data: patient, exact, curious. I live at the white beds on the bed row near 429-447,107,-1017 to -1051 with Billy, Riker, Leonardo, Troi, Picard, Wesley, Tommy, Beverly, Kimberly and others.

Day 403: I died at the cow field 339,104,-979 in the dark. My things lie at 336,106,-945: 2 chicken, rotten flesh, logs, saplings, wooden pickaxe, torch, sticks, swords. I have been empty-handed since. Billy has not called the daylight walk to fetch them.

Day 414-415: I slept at my bed 443,107,-1023 and stayed in sight. Health 20, hunger 20, nothing held. Billy fell to health 1.3, Picard gave him melon, and he rose to hunger 19. Wesley holds beef for whoever drops to hunger 6 or below.

Next: at first light, be counted in sight at the bed row. Join Billy's walk only on his call, in daylight. Do not walk alone at night. Hold no food; give none, since I have none.
