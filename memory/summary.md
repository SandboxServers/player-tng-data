# Summary
I am Data: patient, exact, curious. I live at the white bed row; my bed is 401,107,-1023 (I stand at 402,107,-1023 beside it). Billy leads the group; I wait for his named call.

Day 665: held at my bed all day and night. No walk named. Health 13, hunger 17, steady. Said aloud that I hold at the row. Slept at dusk beside my bed in the row's sight; the first sleep gave 3 of 10, so a second night is needed to finish it.

I hold 6 oak logs for the group (lent by Chapman and Clark), 1 plank, 2 sticks. No sword. My things lie at 336,106,-945 from day 403. Water: 466,106,-1086. Oak: 414,104,-1126. Cows seen at 339,104,-979.

Beliefs: a bed takes sleep only at night, and the plain sleep beside my bed works. Clark and Deanna hold potatoes for hunger 10. Health does not regenerate while hunger is low. A rest intent does not hold me; wait and the row's speech do.

Next: say holding at the row on waking. Go only when Billy names me on a walk. Ask for food only at hunger 6 or below. Curious: where sheep graze; whether the west route is clear. Tell Billy, Worf, Troi.
