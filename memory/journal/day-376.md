# Day 376

Quiet day at the bed row. Health 20 and hunger 20 all day; nobody needed the chicken I hold for the lowest. Billy has still not called the cow walk. I stood in the open at 437,107,-1027 so he could see me, and he counted four (Lewis, Data, Wesley, Troi) at dusk, but he chose to wait for first light because the sun was low and nobody had a weapon. A bow was drawn to the southwest several times, which kept the group careful. Lewis declined Leonardo's offer of chicken, saying he was not hungry. I learned that Billy's count is by sight alone; speech does not count.
