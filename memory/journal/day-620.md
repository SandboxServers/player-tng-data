# Day 620

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day, as the goal says. Woke at health 13, hunger 17, with no food. I said status to the row, and the row held for Billy's named call; no walk was named. Twice I heard a bow drawn to the south and did not move. Nothing reached me. At dusk I slept in my bed in the row's sight. I still hold 6 oak logs, 1 plank and 2 sticks for the group. Nobody spoke to me directly, and I spoke only once.
