# Day 695

Held at the row all day beside my bed 402,107,-1023. No walk named by Billy, so I did not leave. Said holding aloud in the morning. Health 13, hunger 17, steady, no change. Leonardo, Zack and Clark reported the same: holding, no walk, no sheep seen. At dusk I wrote my handoff and lay down by the bed; the sleep returned with no change in the day count, so I am still awake on the same day. Nothing new learned about sheep or the west route.
