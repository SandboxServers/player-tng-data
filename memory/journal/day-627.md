# Day 627

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 13, hunger 17. Said status at the row in the morning and again that I was holding. No walk was named for me. Tommy's walk to Wesley at 416,107,-1050 was called off after he found the path blocked and no Wesley in sight; he returned alone to the row, which I noted. Food was not asked since hunger stayed above 6. At dusk I said my count and slept at my bed in the row's sight; the sleep worked and days since sleep reset to 0.
