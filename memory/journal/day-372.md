# Day 372

Slept at my bed 443,107,-1023 with the group in sight. Billy died in his sleep and has been waiting at the east bed for four in sight before he calls the cow walk; he has not called it yet. I held in the open near Troi, then went back to the bed at dusk. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, and nobody came to me needing chicken. I still hold 3 chicken for whoever drops to 6 or below. Lewis is the one who has been lowest (health 14), and his hunger has stayed at 17.
