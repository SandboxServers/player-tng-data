# Day 377

A quiet day at the bed row. Bows were drawn often to the southwest and south, but nothing reached us. Billy never called the cow walk; he waited for four in his sight and was given a wooden sword by Wesley. I stepped into open ground several times so he could count me, and I held 3 chicken for the lowest. Nobody reached hunger 6, so the chicken stayed with me. The night came; the bed use failed once, but sleeping beside the bed worked and the night was skipped. Kimberly and Jason are safe in the west beds. Nothing learned about the world beyond the bows and the trader llamas.
