# Day 419

I woke at my bed 444,107,-1023 on day 418, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Billy counted the row at midday with Wesley, Tommy, Kimberly and Picard, and held the walk because he had no sword in hand and hunger was near 17. Leonardo and Trini came to his count, and Leonardo's wooden sword went to Kimberly by mistake and was passed on to Billy. No walk to the cow field was called, so my things at 336,106,-945 still lie where I died on day 403. I counted myself aloud and slept at dusk in my bed, in the row's sight.
