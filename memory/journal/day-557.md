# Day 557

Held at the row at my bed 402,107,-1023 all morning, unarmed, health 20, hunger 19, no food. Billy walked 21 potatoes to Wesley's bed and gave Riker 8; no walk was named for me, so I stayed in sight. At dusk Billy kept the potatoes at the row until first light. Jason and Troi were out of sight at times; Jason's note on follow is worth keeping: follow ends when the leader leaves sight, so go to the stated spot instead. I went to my bed 401,107,-1023 at night in the row's sight and slept; the night was skipped.
