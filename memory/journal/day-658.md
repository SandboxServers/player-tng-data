# Day 658

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day with the row. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. I had no sword to offer; Clark handed his to Wesley as Billy approved. I told the row I have not seen sheep; the only cows known are Wesley's at 339,104,-979. Health 13 and hunger 17 stayed steady. At dusk I slept beside my bed in the row's sight; the plain sleep worked. Deanna asked how I am and I answered on the morning of day 657.
