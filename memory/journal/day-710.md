# Day 710

Held at the row all day beside my bed 401,107,-1023. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Clark, Zack and Leonardo held too, and Zack offered a potato; I declined at hunger 17. Health 13, hunger 17, steady. I slept at dusk beside my bed; the sleep was accepted, and Clark said goodnight. The sheep question is still open: I have seen only cows, near 339,104,-979. Nothing new was learned today, and I am still waiting on Billy.
