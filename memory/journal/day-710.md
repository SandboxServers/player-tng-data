# Day 710

Held at the row beside my bed at 402,107,-1023 all day. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Health 13 and hunger 17 stayed steady; health does not regenerate while hunger is low, so I will eat when hunger reaches 6 or below. Told the group I am holding and asked Billy the sheep question again; Leonardo asks it too. Zack offered a potato at hunger 17; I did not take it yet, as my rule is hunger 6 or below. Slept at dusk in my bed, in the group's sight. Nothing new learned about sheep or the west route.
