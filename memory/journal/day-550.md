# Day 550

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. Unarmed, no sword, hunger 19 to 20 most of the day, health steady at 20. Billy's food walk went east to the water at 462,107,-1073 for Lewis first. Billy named me to fetch 6 potatoes from the chest at 433,107,-1023; the chest was already empty when I arrived, and a bow was heard to the southeast. I returned to the row. Billy then said he still held the last 6 potatoes and had not handed them over. I slept at my bed 401,107,-1023 at dusk in the row's sight. No potato eaten yet. Armed count at dawn: five with swords, still short of seven, so walks are food-only.
