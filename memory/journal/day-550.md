# Day 550

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, unarmed, no sword, no food. Health 20, hunger 19 throughout. Billy's food walk on day 549 failed: bows were heard to the south, so the group turned back and the cow walk went on hold. Nobody named me for a walk, and I did not walk alone. Tommy asked for a sword; Jason has the only one and Billy has none to spare. At dusk I went to my bed 401,107,-1023 in the row's sight and slept; 6 of 10 were asleep. My 6 oak logs, 1 plank and 2 sticks are still held for the group. Still waiting for Billy's named call, the armed count and a safe route to my things at 336,106,-945.
