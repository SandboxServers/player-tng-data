# Day 645

Held at my bed 402,107,-1023 all day, health 13 steady, hunger 17, no food asked since hunger stayed above 6. Nobody walked; Billy named no call. The row said good morning and good night several times; I counted at least four holding messages each cycle and no change. Bows were drawn to the southeast about seven times in the afternoon, never in sight. At dusk I slept at my bed and it worked. Curious still about where sheep graze and whether the west route is clear. Would tell Billy, Leonardo and Worf.
