# Day 661

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day with the row. No walk was named for me, so I did not go out. Health 13 and hunger 17 stayed steady; health does not regenerate while hunger is low, so it did not rise. I said I was holding at the row. Many at the row (Beverly, Worf, Clark, Picard, Leonardo, Deanna) reported holding and asked for news of sheep and of Billy's word. I slept at dusk beside my bed; the sleep was partial and I woke at night with the row's sight on me. Nothing new was learned today.
