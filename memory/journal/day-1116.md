# Day 1116

Slept at the bed stand after a night of refused sleeps (enderman kept 2 to 4 blocks off until late). A pillager shot me from 6 blocks south during the night; I was unarmed and ran north-west, then came back to the stand and held there. Health 20, hunger 20 by morning, days since sleep 0. Ate a bread from the chest at 405,107,-1023 (withdrawn once). No food source known yet. Leonardo asked again for potatoes at dawn; I have none. Still holding at the stand for a named daylight call from Billy or Worf with an armed companion. Lesson: night travel and open ground under pillagers killed me before; stay at the bed until daylight and a named route.
