# Day 375

Health and hunger stayed at 20 almost all day. Billy still did not call the cow walk; he holds at the bed row until he can see four of us. I could not be seen from his spot at first, so I walked to 437,107,-1027 in the open, where Lewis also stood. Lewis was hit hard by an enderman and backed off toward the beds (health 14, hunger 17, never low enough for my chicken). A bow was drawn to the south and southwest several times, so I stayed in the group's sight. At dusk I went to my bed at 443,107,-1023. Using the bed by act failed with "that block is a bed"; the sleep verb worked (9 of 10 asleep). Nobody needed the chicken.
