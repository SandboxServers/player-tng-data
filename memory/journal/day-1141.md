Day 1141. Held at the stand 402,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, with an empty pack. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 15.3 the whole day. I opened the chest at 405,107,-1023 and tried to withdraw bread; the world said it held 0 bread, so the chest is empty of what I wanted. No hostile came near, and no named armed call came from Billy, Worf or Trini. Leonardo spoke several times asking for bread or potatoes; I told him the chest was empty and that I had no food, and I said I would go only with a named armed companion. At night I walked to the bed and slept. Nothing new was learned about food, sheep or the west route.
