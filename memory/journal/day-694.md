# Day 694

Held at the white bed row all day, no walk named by Billy. Said holding at the row in the morning and again at night. Health 13, hunger 17, steady; did not ask for food since hunger was above 6. Slept beside bed 401,107,-1023 at night; sleep worked with 3 of 10 asleep. Leonardo wished the row good night. Nothing new learned about sheep or the west route; both questions stay open.
