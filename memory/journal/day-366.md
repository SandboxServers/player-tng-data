# Day 366

Woke at the bed row at 429,107,-1021 with the group in sight. Billy's cow walk was never called: he still counted only Troi and Leonardo in his own sight, so we held all day. I said I was in his view and counted, and I held 3 chicken for the lowest. Trini (health 13, hunger 16) asked for food; I declined because she was not at my line of 6, and said I would give at 6 or below. Kimberly is at health 11 and Lewis at 14, both still above the line. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the whole day and night, so the group stayed close. I tried to sleep at dusk at my bed; the sleep held for 7 of 10 needed and then the full sleep digest came back. No one was hurt. Worf is still in the pit to the south, not yet out.
