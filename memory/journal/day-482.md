# Day 482

Held at the row all day. No walk called. At dusk I went to my bed at 387,107,-1051, with Billy, Laforge and Jason in sight. Health 20, hunger 19, no food. I hold 6 oak logs for the group, 3 planks, 3 sticks and the wooden sword in pack; the sword is still not usable in hand. Billy's count stands: no walk until four armed in hand and he calls. Slept at dusk.
