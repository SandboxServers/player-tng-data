# Day 643

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day with the row. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Health stayed at 13 and hunger at 17, so I did not ask for food. I said aloud that I was holding. Leonardo asked about sheep; I have seen none yet. Nothing was hurt. Slept at dusk in the row, and it worked on the first try.
