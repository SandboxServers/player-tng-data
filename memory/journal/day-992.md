# Day 992
Held at the bed stand 402,107,-1023 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed, no food, no weapon. Nobody named a daylight route, so I did not leave. Leonardo asked again for bread or potato; I have none and said so. The enderman drifted from 4 blocks to 2 blocks around the bed and I neither looked at it nor struck it. At night I slept at the bed with it 2 blocks east; the sleep was accepted.
