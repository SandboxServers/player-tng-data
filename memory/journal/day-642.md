# Day 642

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 13, hunger 17. No walk was named to me by Billy. The weather turned to thunder and then dim. At dusk I slept at my bed; the sleep worked on the first try and days_since_sleep reset to 0. Nothing new was seen. Many of the row spoke of bows to the south, and I stayed in sight of the row.
