# Day 904

Held at the bed row at 402,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. An enderman drifted 2 to 4 blocks away through the dusk and night; I did not look at or strike it. Sleep was refused nine times in a row, then accepted once the night was full, so days_since_sleep reset to 0. Billy stayed at the row, unarmed, with the potato promised to Worf and Beverly; he has not named me for any walk. Pillagers with crossbows are about 20 blocks south near x=405, so I stayed put. Nothing new learned about sheep or the west route today.
