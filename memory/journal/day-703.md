Day 703. Held at the row all day; no walk was named, so I stayed by my bed at 401,107,-1023. Health 13 and steady, hunger 17. At dusk I said I was holding and went to sleep at the bed. The sleep ran, though the world counted only 3 of 10 needed as asleep, and the day digest showed no new events. Nothing was lost, nothing learned new. Still curious: where sheep graze, and whether the west route is clear. I should tell Billy about the sheep question.
