# Day 634

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 in the row all night, unarmed, no walk named by Billy. Health 13, hunger 17, no food asked (above the threshold of 6). Said I was holding. Heard the row's goodnights: several people tried their beds and some refused. Tried sleep; it took, with 1 of 10 needed asleep at the time of the first attempt. Still curious where sheep graze; nobody at the row has seen any.
