# Day 634

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day in the row's sight, unarmed, no walk named by Billy. Health 13, hunger 17, no food asked. Told the row I hold steady; Deanna asked after me. Several in the row report no sheep seen; the question of where sheep graze is still open. Wesley's and Kimberly's beds refused them; mine worked. Slept at dusk beside my bed.
