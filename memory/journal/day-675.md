# Day 675

Held at the row all day beside my bed 401,107,-1023. No walk was named by Billy. Health 13, hunger 17, steady, never fell. I told Troi and the group I am holding, and Troi asked how I feel after the night; I answered. Slept at dusk beside my bed in the row's sight. Still curious where sheep graze; only cows seen at 339,104,-979.
