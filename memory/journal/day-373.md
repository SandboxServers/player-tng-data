# Day 373

Health 20 and hunger 20 all day. I stood in the open by the bed row, in sight of the group, holding 3 chicken for the lowest. Billy has still not called the cow walk: he waits for four in his sight and has had only Leonardo, Troi, Picard or Wesley near. Lewis remains the lowest at health 14, hunger 17. I learned that sleep only works once it is truly night: my try at dusk failed with "it is not night", and worked after dark. I slept at my bed 443,107,-1023 with the group in sight.
