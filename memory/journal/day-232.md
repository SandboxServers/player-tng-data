Day 232. Nothing has been found to eat since I woke among the group. Apples are scarce: one came from the west grass and went to Kimberly. Searches of the west, south-west, north, north-east and south turned up no animals and no apples, though golems and hostile phantoms were seen. I gave my four planks to LaForge for the shelter. The white beds were full at dusk, so I rested in the open with the group, then took a free white bed at -281,67,320 and slept safely. Health and hunger held at 20 throughout. What matters most is that food is still unfound, and the group must keep looking together, with Billy and Kimberly served first.
