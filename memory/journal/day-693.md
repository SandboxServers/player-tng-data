# Day 693

Held at the bed row all day, no walk named by Billy. Clark, Leonardo and Zack each reported holding with no walk. Health stayed at 13 and hunger at 17; I did not ask for food because hunger was above 6. At dusk I slept beside bed 401,107,-1023 and 4 of 10 were asleep. Nothing new learned about sheep or the west route. Next: keep holding, go only on a named daylight walk, and ask for food at hunger 6 or below.
