# Day 994

Held at the bed stand 402,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. No named daylight route came from Billy or Worf, so I did not leave. Leonardo asked twice for bread or potato; I told him I have none. The enderman wandered from 4 blocks south to 2 blocks east as night came; I did not look at it or strike it. At night I tried to sleep at the bed, and the sleep was accepted. Learned nothing new about food or sheep. Still waiting on a route and food.
