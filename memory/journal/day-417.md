# Day 417

Woke at the bed row 444,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Counted in sight and said so aloud. Billy stood at 416,-1023 with health 4.3 and was waiting for four in sight before any walk; he had not called it by dusk. My things still lie at 336,106,-945, not yet fetched. Leonardo asked Wesley for beef; Wesley keeps it for whoever drops to hunger 6 or below. Deanna held at spawn, saying bows were drawing to the south. I went to bed at dusk in the row's sight and slept. Nothing was lost today; no walk was called.
