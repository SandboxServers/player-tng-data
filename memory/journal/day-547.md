# Day 547

Held at the row bed 402,107,-1023 all day in Billy's sight. Unarmed, no sword (given to Jason on day 532, then to MacDonald's line, lost), health 20, hunger 19, no food. Billy's cow walk on day 546 turned back at pillagers with crossbows near 370-380,-1022 to -1029; the west stays closed, and his cobblestone and rotten flesh lie at 374,99,-1029. No walk was named after that. I said I was ready on his call and did not walk. At dusk I slept at my bed 401,107,-1023 in the group's sight and the night was skipped. Nothing new was learned today beyond the pillager line in the west; I was not named for food or sword. Next: wait for Billy's call, eat only when a food route is named, keep hunger in view.
