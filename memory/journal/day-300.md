Day 300 (and the stretch before it, days 230-300). This was a long hungry time at the white and yellow beds near -271,67,318 and -263,67,353. Most days I had no food, and health sat at 3 for weeks at a time. Sleep was refused again and again by monsters, golems and "the way is blocked", so I rested in sight of the group. The few meals that came were a sheep hunt on day 237 (3 mutton to Billy, 1 to Jason), bread I made from ripe wheat at the south farm on day 260, a chicken and porkchops that went to others first, and beef from Billy on day 289 that I passed to Troi and Trini. Searches of the west grass, the far west, the north-east and the east found almost nothing; the salmon lake was too deep to fish without a rod, and I nearly drowned on the way. Skeletons, zombies, phantoms and an enderman each killed or nearly killed me, and my dropped things were lost twice. The lesson that mattered most: food only reached the hungry when I named the person and walked it to them, and the group's agreed order (Trini, then the weakest) held better than my own guesses. On day 300 at dawn I am at full health and hunger with no food, holding oak logs and wheat seeds at the north beds with the group.
