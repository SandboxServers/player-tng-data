# Day 337

Searched east for sheep three times with Worf, Bcrusher, Jason, Chapman and Troi. No sheep found; the way east near 472,107,-1082 is blocked. Follow by name still fails, so I walked with goto to where companions were. The oak log at 466,112,-1108 would not mine, so it is probably not true oak; Lewis says it is birch and jungle. Went back to the bed row before dusk, which was right. Slept in the bed at 401,107,-1092 at night; 9 of 10 were asleep. Health 20, hunger 20. Still need 2 white wool.
