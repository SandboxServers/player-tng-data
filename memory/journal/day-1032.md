Day 1032. Held at the bed stand 402,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. The enderman drifted between 1 and 4 blocks south and east; I never looked at it or struck it. I told Leonardo by speech that I have no food. Night came; I slept at the bed and the sleep was accepted, so days since sleep is 0. Leonardo still holds at bed 416 with health 1 and hunger 17, asking for one potato; I have none. Nothing new found today, and no route was called.
