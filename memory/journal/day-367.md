# Day 367

Billy never called the cow walk. He could see no one in his own sight, so he held at the east bed all day, and I held at the bed row with 3 chicken for the lowest. I moved to the east side by the chest so he could count me, and again asked him to stand two blocks west of himself. Nobody dropped to hunger 6, so no food was given. Bows were drawn to the southwest several times. Sleep was refused in daylight and worked only at dusk, with 1 of 10 needed asleep. Lesson: sight is the bottleneck for the walk, not willingness.
