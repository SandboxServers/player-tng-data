# Day 668

Held at the row all day with no walk named by Billy. Said holding at the row on waking, and again at dusk. Health 13, hunger 17, steady throughout. The row spoke good nights and mornings: Troi and Deanna, Leonardo, Jason, Clark, Lewis, Worf. Nobody walked. I slept beside my bed at 401,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Still open: where sheep graze (only cows seen, at 339,104,-979), and whether the west route is clear. Tell Billy, Worf, Troi.
