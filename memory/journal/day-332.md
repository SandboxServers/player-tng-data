# Day 332

Today I stayed at the bed row near 385,-1083 through midday, hoping to search for sheep south-west with Riker. Riker came close only at the end of the day, and no sheep were seen anywhere in my sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Others (Chapman, Troi, Kimberly, Laforge, Bcrusher) walked to the cow spot at 370,93,-1124 in daylight; Troi still wanted to visit her drops at 408,-1050, which I declined. At dusk I walked to the bed at 387,107,-1093 with Riker beside me and slept. I still hold 1 white wool and need 2 more to craft a white bed.
