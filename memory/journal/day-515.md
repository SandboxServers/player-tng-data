# Day 515

Held at the white row 402,107,-1023 all day, unarmed in hand, wooden sword in pack, hunger 19, health 20 throughout. Billy named Zack and me to hold near the row; no walk to the cows until morning. Four armed in hand (Billy, Tommy, Chapman, Laforge). Trini's stone sword went to Chapman; Clark's sword went by mistake to Tommy, then Trini, then Chapman, so Leonardo still lacks one. Nothing was lost. At dusk I went to my bed at 401,107,-1023, in the row's sight, and slept. Still holding 6 oak logs for the group.
