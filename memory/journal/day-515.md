# Day 515

Dusk at the white row, 402,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 19, no food. The group held at the row all day under Billy's call: no walks, no cow field. Four armed by the count; the wooden sword passed around by mistake from Chapman to Clark, Tommy, Trini and back to Leonardo, and it is still not settled who holds it. Laforge cannot say how his sword got into his hand. I kept my wooden sword in pack and stayed unarmed in hand, as my goals say. Nothing was asked of me, so I gave nothing. I walked to my bed at 401,107,-1023 in the group's sight and slept there. Nine of ten needed were asleep at the time of the note.
