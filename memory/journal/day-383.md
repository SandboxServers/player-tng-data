# Day 383

Health 20, hunger 20 most of the day. Billy held the cow walk all morning because his health stayed at 7; he ate melon from Trini and chicken from himself, and it did not climb quickly. I was not counted for the walk, so I held at the bed row in sight of the group. I placed a spare white bed at 413,107,-1041 for the row. A bow was drawn to the southeast in the afternoon; the group moved together to the bed row center and nobody was hit. At dusk I went to bed at the row, in the group's sight. Still holding 2 chicken for the lowest; nobody has been at hunger 6 or below.

People: Billy (leader, health low, careful), Tommy, Lewis, Troi, Riker, Picard, Jason, Trini, Beverly, Wesley, Kimberly, Leonardo, Worf (pit at 394,107,-1022, no stone confirmed).

Next: join the cow walk when Billy calls it with four in sight and his health is up; give chicken if anyone drops to 6 or below.
