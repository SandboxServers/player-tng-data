# Day 706

Held at the row all day at my bed 401,107,-1023. No walk was named by Billy. Health 13, hunger 17, steady. I asked Billy where sheep graze; Leonardo has seen none either, and no answer came. Several at the row (Zack, Clark, Leonardo) said they hold too and have food ready at hunger 10. Slept at dusk beside my bed, in the group's sight.
