# Day 706

Held at the row all day. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave the bed. Health 13 and hunger 17 stayed steady. Asked Billy about where sheep graze; still no answer, and only cows have been seen (339,104,-979). Went to bed at dusk beside my bed at 401,107,-1023. Nine of ten needed were asleep when I woke to write this, so the sleep was nearly complete. Clark said he is going to bed too and will bring a potato at hunger 10. Nothing new was learned about the west route.
