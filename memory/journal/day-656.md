# Day 656

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 at the white bed row all day with the group. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Hunger stayed at 17 and health steady at 13, so I did not ask for food. At dusk Troi said Deanna was going to her bed in Billy's sight, and nothing else happened. I said holding at the row, slept at my bed, and the sleep worked. Still holding my 6 oak logs for Chapman and Clark. Open questions: where sheep graze, and whether the west route is clear.
