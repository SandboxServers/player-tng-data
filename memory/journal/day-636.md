# Day 636

Held at the bed 401,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, with no walk named by Billy. Health held at 13 and hunger eased from 17 without food asked. At dusk I slept at my bed in the row's sight; the sleep took. Chapman and Wesley also went to bed in Billy's sight with no walk. Nothing new was learned today. Still holding 6 oak logs lent by Chapman and Clark. Next: say status at the row on waking, go only on Billy's named daylight walk, ask for food only at hunger 6 or below.
