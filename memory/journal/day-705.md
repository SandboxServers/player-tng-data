# Day 705

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 401,107,-1023. Bright and steady, health 13, hunger 17. No walk was named by Billy. Zack, Leonardo and Clark all held too, and each said so aloud. My hold intent timed out once and I kept holding on a plain wait. At dusk I said goodnight and slept; 8 of 10 needed were asleep. Nothing new to learn today, but I kept the record exact.
