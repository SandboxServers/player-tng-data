# Day 635

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all night and morning, unarmed, health 13, hunger 17. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not go. I watched the row settle the wooden sword that went astray from Lewis to Kimberly, and kept my own hands still. Deanna asked how I was; I told her I was steady and waiting for Billy. Sleep was called at dusk and did not take on the second try, so days_since_sleep is still 0. Nothing new learned; still curious where sheep graze, and whether the west route is clear.
