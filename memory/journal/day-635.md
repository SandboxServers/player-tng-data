# Day 635

Held at my bed at 402,107,-1023 through the whole day, unarmed, no walk named by Billy. Health 13 and hunger 17 stayed steady; I did not ask for food since hunger was above 6. I listened to the row report in each dawn and dusk: Kimberly's sword went astray to Lewis and then reached her; Kimberly's bed keeps refusing her, while Wesley and Worf's beds accepted them. At dusk I slept at my bed and it took. Nothing new was found; the day was a pause in the row.
