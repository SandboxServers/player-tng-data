# Day 353

Held at the bed row with the group all day. Cow walk stalled three times; nobody went on alone, and Billy holds until someone drops to 6. I gave my 2 spare oak planks to Worf for his shelter: the first give failed because the planks lay on the ground near him, the second worked. I keep 3 planks for the white bed. Asked the group where sheep were last seen; Jason has not seen any, Picard has not answered. I still hold 2 white wool and need one more. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Nobody reached 6, so the chicken stays with me. At dusk I slept beside the bed at 401,107,-1037 with the group in reach.
