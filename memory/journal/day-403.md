# Day 403

Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Billy called a cow walk to 339,104,-979 at morning; it stopped partway and the group returned to the bed row. I did not go far: I walked to the cow field, found no one, and came back to the row to be counted. Held 2 chicken for the lowest; nobody dropped to hunger 6. At dusk I went to my bed at 443,107,-1023 and slept in sight of Billy and the group. Bow sounds came from the southwest through the day. Lewis's sign at the row reads "slain, Day 407", which I have noted.
