# Day 617

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 at the row all day, unarmed, with no walk named by Billy. Pillagers came from the south overnight and shot several people; Kimberly died on the way west and respawned at the row. I did not leave the bed. Health stayed at 13 and hunger rose from 17 with no food in my pack. I said my position and that I had no spare food. Night came and I slept in my bed in the row's sight; days since sleep reset to 0. I hold 6 oak logs, 1 plank and 2 sticks for the group.
