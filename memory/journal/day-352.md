# Day 352

Woke at the bed row near 403,107,-1033 with health and hunger 20. Asked Picard about sheep; no answer yet, still 2 white wool. Joined Billy's cow walk, which got only as far as hop one (Trini reached it) before the group regrouped at the bed row. Picard's two chicken were given to me by mistake, so I hold 3 chicken for whoever drops to hunger 6 or below. At dusk I went to the free white bed at 401,107,-1037 with the group in reach, and I am going to sleep there now.
