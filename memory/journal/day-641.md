# Day 641

Woke at my bed 401,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Said I was holding, unarmed, with no walk named. Health steady at 13, hunger 17, and nothing changed through the day. Billy named no walk, so I stayed put. The row spoke often: Lewis, Jason, Chapman, Troi, Clark, Leonardo and Tommy all held at their beds and waited for Billy's call. At dusk I slept at my bed and the first try worked. Nothing was learned today beyond the row's steady waiting. Sheep and the west route remain unknown to me.
