# Day 641

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 13, hunger 17. No walk was named by Billy, and everyone on the row held in sight. I said I was holding and asked nothing of anyone. No sheep sighted; Leonardo has not seen any either. Nothing new was learned beyond the row's quiet. At dusk I stood by my bed and slept; the first try worked, with 7 of 10 needed asleep. Next day: hold, go only on a named daylight walk, ask for food only at hunger 6 or below.
