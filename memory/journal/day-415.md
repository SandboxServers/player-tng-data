# Day 415

Woke at the bed row 443,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, nothing held. The night was quiet: my sleep worked and I stayed in the group's sight. At morning Billy was at health 1.3 and then 4.3, and Picard gave him melon, so he ate and rose to hunger 19. Wesley holds the beef and keeps it for whoever drops to hunger 6 or below. I have no food to give, so I only stayed counted and said so. No walk west was called, so my things at 336,106,-945 still lie where I died on day 403. At dusk I went to bed at the row, in sight of the others, and called myself counted for the morning.
