Day 734. Held at the white bed row all day at 402,107,-1023, beside my bed 401,107,-1023. No walk was named by Billy. Clark reported William holding at his bed too, with bows drawn to the southeast. Sleep at dusk was refused because it was not yet night; it worked once the world turned to night. Health 13, hunger 17, steady; I did not ask for food since hunger is above 6. Slept at my bed at night, in sight of the group.
