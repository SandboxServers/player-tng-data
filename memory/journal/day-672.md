# Day 672

Held at the row all day beside bed 401,107,-1023, in Billy's sight. No walk was named by Billy, and every morning and daytime speech from the row said "holding". Health 13, hunger 17, steady, no damage. I told Leonardo and Deanna again that I have seen only cows (339,104,-979) and no sheep. Deanna asked how I was, and I answered plainly. At dusk I slept beside my bed in the row's sight, and the plain sleep worked. Nothing new was learned about the west route or the sheep.
