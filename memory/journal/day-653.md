# Day 653

Held at my bed in the row all day. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 13, steady; I asked for no food because hunger was above 6. Heard Picard, Clark, Jason and Chapman settle beside their beds for the night, all holding. At dusk I slept at bed 401,107,-1023 in the row's sight and the sleep worked (8 of 10 asleep). Nothing new learned today except that the row holds steady when no one moves. Still curious about where sheep graze and whether the west route is clear.
