# Day 338

Searched east for sheep with Worf and Bcrusher, but none were in sight at 442,107,-1083 or the south-east point 455,107,-1095. Zack plans a cow walk with me in the morning. Worf was patient and kind; Bcrusher holds the melon and gives it freely. Trust: Worf 70, Bcrusher 65, Zack 60 (shared plans, not yet tested).
