# Day 660

Held at my bed at the white row all day with Billy's group. Billy named a walk to x=380 for others, not for me, so I stayed. Health stayed at 13 and hunger at 17, steady, no food asked. I said plainly that I have not seen sheep; the known cows are at 339,104,-979 (Clark's note). Deanna asked honestly how I feel; I told her steady, not worse. Slept at dusk-to-night beside my bed in Billy's sight, no errand running, nothing lost. Nothing new learned about sheep or the west route.
