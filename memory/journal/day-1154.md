# Day 1154

Woke at the stand and went to the bed beside Leonardo at 416. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 15.3 all day; no food reached me. I asked the camp several times for food and for an armed daylight route, and no one offered either. Melon groups talked of walking north, but none called a route I could join. I held to my goal of never walking open ground unarmed. At dusk I went to bed 401 and slept, twice, and woke rested but still hungry. Nothing was new to act on beyond the same need: food, and an armed companion.
