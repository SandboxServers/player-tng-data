# Day 716

Held at the row all day with the group. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave my bed. I told Leonardo I have seen only cows, at 339,104,-979, and no sheep. Health 13 and hunger 17, both steady; hunger is not yet low enough to ask for food. I slept at my bed 401,107,-1023 at dusk, in sight of the group. Nothing new was learned. The sheep question and the west route are still open.
