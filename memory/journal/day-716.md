# Day 716

Held at the row all day beside my bed 401,107,-1023. No walk was named by Billy; Leonardo, Clark and Zack are all waiting on the same word. Health stayed at 13 and hunger at 17, steady. Nobody came near me on the morning waking except by speech. At dusk I said goodnight at the row and slept in sight of the group. Nothing new learned about sheep; still only cows seen at 339,104,-979.
