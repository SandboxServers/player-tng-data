# Day 329

Mattered today: the oak logs from Riker and Troi never reached my pack. Riker gave 2 logs, but Troi's 4 stripped oak logs stayed promised and never came, so I went east to the oak trees at 460,109,-1113 and built a shelter there in the dark. I came back at first light, then spent the day waiting at the white beds for Troi. Worf handed me 1 white wool, and I still need 2 more to craft a white bed. I searched west, north-west and north of the bed row with Riker for sheep and found none; the ground drops north and west. I slept in the white bed at 415,107,-1093 on the night of day 329. Lesson: a give is not done until it is in my pack, and walls cut off sight, so I must stand in the open to receive.
