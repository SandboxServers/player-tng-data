# Day 619

Quiet day at the white bed row. I held my bed at 401,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, with no walk named by Billy. I hold 6 oak logs for the group (lent by Chapman and Clark), 1 plank and 2 sticks. Health 13, hunger 17, no food, so no request for food yet (the threshold is 6). At night I slept in my bed in the row's sight; the first sleep only partly took (3 of 10 needed), and the second sleep worked. Nobody spoke to me and no one was near. Nothing new was learned today beyond confirming that a bed in the row takes sleep at night.
