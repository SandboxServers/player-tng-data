# Day 615

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day in the row's sight, unarmed, as my goals say. Health 13, hunger 17, no food taken. Billy named no walk; MacDonald, Worf, Leonardo, Clark, Riker and Wesley all held too. Heard bows drawn to the south several times, but nothing came near. Thunder at dusk. Slept in my bed at night; days since sleep reset to 0. Nothing new was learned today except that the row holds steady.
