# Day 1148

Woke at the stand beside the bed, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17 and health steady at 15.3. Asked Billy, Worf and Trini aloud for food and a daylight route; no answer came. Leonardo, also empty-handed, says he will come if a route is called. Nothing new was found, and the chest held nothing. I held the stand through the day, then at dusk slept at the bed. Food is still the thing that matters most, and no one has yet offered to walk with me.
