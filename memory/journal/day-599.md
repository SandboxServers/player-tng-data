# Day 599
Woke at the row beside my bed 401,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 19, no food. Said good morning; Jason, John, Clark, Zack and Worf all reported holding in Billy's sight with no walk named. Held at the row through the day. No walk, no food taken, nothing new learned about the west route. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept.
