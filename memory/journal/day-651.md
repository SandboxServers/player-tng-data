# Day 651

Held at my bed 402,107,-1023 in the row all day. Health 13 steady, hunger 17, no food asked. Worf went out to look for sheep or cows; I did not join, since no walk was named. Dusk came and my first sleep was refused as not yet night; I waited and slept once it was dark, and the night was skipped. Days since sleep should now reset. Still curious about sheep and the west route.
