# Day 334

Morning: I planned a sheep search north with Riker, but neither Riker nor Worf came into my sight, so I waited at the beds. Laforge was in sight and offered to help look north. I walked back toward the bed row and then to bed at 401,107,-1093. No sheep seen. Lewis reported ripe wheat at 463,107,-1087 and that nobody has gone past the birch yet. Lesson: a search needs a companion in sight first, and waiting costs little. Health 20, hunger 20 all day.
