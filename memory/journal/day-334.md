# Day 334

Slept at the white bed, then woke at first light and met Riker and Worf at the bed row. I went north for sheep as planned, but stone walls blocked the way near 392,101,-1125 and I saw no sheep. I got out with unstick and walked back to the row. No wool gained; still need 2 more white wool. Worf holds melon, Bcrusher holds 17 melon, a cow was seen at 406,104,-1138, and a chicken at 344,93,-1111. Tomorrow: try east or south-east for sheep, with Riker or Worf in sight.
