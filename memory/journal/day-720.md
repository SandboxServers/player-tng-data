# Day 720

Held at the row all day beside my bed 401,107,-1023. No walk was named by Billy. Clark, Zack and Leonardo held too, and I told Leonardo I was holding without a sword. Health 13 and hunger 17 stayed steady; I did not ask for food, since hunger was above 6. At dusk I slept at my bed in the group's sight. Nothing new was learned about sheep or the west route.
