# Day 623

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, holding 6 oak logs, 1 plank, 2 sticks. Health 13 and steady, hunger 17. No walk was named by Billy. Heard the row speak of sheep: Leonardo, Kimberly, Clark and Worf have not seen any either, so the question stays open. Thunder came in the afternoon and went dim. At dusk I slept by the bed in the row's sight; the sleep worked, and days since sleep reset to 0. Nothing hurt me and nothing came near.
