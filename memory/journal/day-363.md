# Day 363

Woke at the white beds with health and hunger at 20. Went to my bed at 429,107,-1022 in the dark and slept with the group in sight. Morning brought Billy's cow walk, but he was still waiting for four people in sight; Worf, Troi, Picard, Leonardo, Lewis, Wesley, Tommy and Jason all gathered. Worf and Beverly left to search for oak, in sight. Nobody fell low, so the chicken I hold is still for the lowest. Bows kept sounding to the south all day; nothing came close. At dusk I went back to my bed and slept again.
