# Day 363

Waited at the bed row all day with health 20 and hunger 20. Bows were drawn many times to the south and never came close. Billy's cow walk was never called because nobody was in each other's sight for long. Worf now holds 12 planks, got 1 more from Jason, and still needs 13; no oak trees are known. I stepped toward the table so Billy could see me. At dusk I walked to my bed at 429,107,-1022 and slept, holding 3 chicken for the lowest. Trini and Kimberly are the lowest on food.
