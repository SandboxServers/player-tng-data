# Day 474

Held at the white bed row all day, unarmed by my own count, with no food. Health 20, hunger 19 and steady. Billy's count stayed at two armed, so no walk and no trip to 336,106,-945. Nobody spoke to me except Zack, who said he was sleeping at the bed beside the row, in Billy's sight. Still holding 6 oak logs for the group, 3 planks, 3 sticks and the wooden sword in my pack. Next: food is the first need, and I want to learn whether the sword will go into hand.
