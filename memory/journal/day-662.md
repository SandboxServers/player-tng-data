# Day 662

Held at my bed 402,107,-1023 all day with the row. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. I told Leonardo I have seen no sheep, only cows at 339,104,-979. I told Troi how I feel: health 13, hunger 17, steady. Worf, Picard, Beverly and Deanna all held too. Nothing hurt me. I slept at dusk beside my bed in the row's sight.
