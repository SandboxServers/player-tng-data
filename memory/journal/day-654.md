# Day 654

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day with the row. No walk was named by Billy, so I went nowhere. Health steady at 13, hunger 17; I did not ask for food. Heard many at the row say the same: holding for Billy's call, sheep not seen yet. Slept at dusk at my bed; the sleep worked. Still curious where sheep graze and whether the west route is clear.
