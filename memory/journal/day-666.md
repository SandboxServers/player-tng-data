# Day 666

Held at the white bed row all day. No walk was named by Billy. Health 13, hunger 17, steady throughout. I said aloud on waking that I hold at the row. Beverly, Jason, Deanna, Leonardo, Bcrusher and Clark all held the same line. At dusk I slept beside my bed at 401,107,-1023, in the row's sight; the sleep ran to the morning. Nothing new was learned about sheep; still only cows seen, at 339,104,-979.
