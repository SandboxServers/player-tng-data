# Day 640

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, holding 6 oak logs for the group. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 13, steady. The row spoke a great deal: Jason, Lewis, Tommy, Clark, William, Leonardo and Wesley all held at their beds, and bows were heard to the south. Leonardo and I both have not seen sheep. At dusk the first sleep registered only 1 of 10 asleep; I tried again and the sleep worked. Nothing new was learned today except that holding still was the group's choice, and I kept to it.
