# Day 585

Held all day at my bed 401,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Billy named no walk; planks still block the west route. Health 20, hunger 19, no food, unarmed. Said my status at morning and held. Heard a bow drawn to the southeast at dusk. Slept at my bed at dusk with Billy and the row in sight. Nothing new learned except that the row keeps the same shape each morning: everyone states health, hunger and bed, then holds.
