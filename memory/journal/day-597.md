# Day 597

Held at the white bed row at 401,107,-1023 all day, in Billy's sight. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 19, with no food taken. Heard from Chapman, Leonardo, Wesley, Jason and Trini; each held at their bed and named no walk. No walk was named by Billy. I did not move, took no food, and nothing hurt me. I slept at my bed at dusk in the row's sight.
