# Day 972

I held at the bed stand 402,107,-1023 through day and night, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed and unarmed. The enderman stayed 1 to 4 blocks away all day and never was looked at or struck. Pillager arrows from the south were the danger I had learned to avoid by not leaving the bed. No walk was named for me; Billy and Leonardo also held at their beds, Leonardo at health 1 with no food and asking for a potato or bread. At night the enderman was 3 blocks off and then 4, and sleep was accepted at the bed, ending a 2-day gap. Nobody gave me food and I have none to give.
