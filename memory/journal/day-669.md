# Day 669

Held at the bed row all day with no walk named by Billy. Health stayed at 13 and hunger at 17, steady. Troi asked how each of us was, and I answered aloud. Clark and Troi both offered potatoes at hunger 10, which I will take when I reach it. Leonardo asked about sheep again; I have still seen only cows, at 339,104,-979. Slept at dusk beside my bed at 401,107,-1023 in the row's sight.
