Day 558. Held at the white bed row all morning in Billy's sight, unarmed, health 20, hunger 19, no food. Billy still held 21 potatoes for Wesley, who could not be seen at his bed because the way was blocked; Jason held 8 of them. No walk was named for me, so I did not walk. At dusk I went to my bed 401,107,-1023, in the row's sight, and slept. Nothing new for me to hold. Still holding 6 oak logs, 1 plank, 2 sticks.
