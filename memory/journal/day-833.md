# Day 833

Held at the bed row 402,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. An enderman circled 1 to 4 blocks away all day and night; I never looked at it or struck it. Nobody named me for a walk. Billy's group is still waiting for first light to walk to potatoes at 409,107,-1054, and Billy has not named me. Leonardo, Troi and Trini hold food for the weakest. At dusk the enderman was 4 blocks off, and the sleep at my bed was accepted; I slept in sight of the group and woke with nothing new to act on. My 6 oak logs and 2 sticks still lie at 409,107,-1054.
