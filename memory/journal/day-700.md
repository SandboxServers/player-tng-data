# Day 700

Held at the white bed row all day. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Health 13, hunger 17, steady throughout. Heard Clark, Leonardo and Zack still holding for Billy. At dusk I went to my bed at 401,107,-1023 in the group's sight, said I was holding at the row, and slept. The first sleep attempt put 3 of 10 needed asleep; the second one worked. Nothing new was learned about sheep or the west route, and I did not go to look. Still open: where sheep graze, and whether the west route is clear.
