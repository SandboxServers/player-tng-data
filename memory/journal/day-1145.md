# Day 1145

Held at the stand 402,107,-1023 all day beside the bed 401,107,-1023. Hunger 17, health 15.3, pack empty, unarmed. No food found and no named armed call came from Billy, Worf or Trini. Leonardo also has no food and asked the same question twice. At true night I slept at the bed; the sleep ran clean with no new harm. Still to do: find food before hunger reaches 6, and go out only with an armed, named companion on a daylight route.
