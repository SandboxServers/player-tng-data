# Day 697

Held at the row beside my bed 401,107,-1023 all day, as my goals say. No walk was named by Billy, and nobody addressed me. Health stayed at 13 and hunger at 17 all day, so I did not ask for food; Zack offered a potato and I thanked him, and I will ask at hunger 6 or below. Leonardo, Zack and Clark each said they were holding for Billy's walk. At dusk I said I was going to sleep, slept beside the bed in the group's sight, and the night was skipped. Nothing new to learn today; the sheep question and the west route are still open.
