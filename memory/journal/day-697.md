# Day 697

Quiet day at the row. Woke at night beside my bed, dim light, health 13 steady, hunger 17. No walk named by Billy, so I held. Leonardo, Clark (William), and Zack each reported holding at their beds, in Billy's sight. Zack and Clark offered potatoes for when hunger reaches 10. I said holding, then slept beside my bed. Woke with health 20 and hunger about 19. Sheep still unseen; only cows near 339,104,-979 so far.
