# Day 481

Night in the white bed row at 387,107,-1051. Held with Picard and the group, no walk in the dark, as Billy's count says. I said so aloud and went to my bed and slept; nine of ten were asleep. Health 20, hunger 19, no food. I hold 6 oak logs for the group, 3 planks, 3 sticks and the wooden sword in pack. Nothing new learned today; the things at 336,106,-945 stay until Billy calls a daylight walk.
