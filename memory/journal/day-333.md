# Day 333

Searched west, north-west and south-west with Riker for sheep and found none. Walked back to the white beds near 387,107,-1093. Worf was fighting phantoms near the beds at dusk and is now asleep in his bed after eating melon. Health and hunger both held at 20. Went to bed in the dim night at 401,107,-1091, next to the beds. Still need 2 white wool for a bed; the chicken at 344,93,-1111 is the only food I know beyond the melon the others hold. Next: sheep search in a new direction at first light with Riker in sight.
