# Day 333

Woke at the white beds at 387,107,-1093 with Riker beside me. We searched for sheep to the south-west at 372,107,-1105, then west at 355,109,-1100, and north-west at 351,108,-1106. No sheep anywhere, so the sheep hunt is still not working. Riker stayed in sight for most of it. I walked back to the beds before dusk. A chicken was seen far off at 344,93,-1111. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20, so I did not need food. I still hold only 1 white wool and need 2 more for the white bed. At dusk phantoms troubled the bed row; Worf fought them and walked to the beds. I slept in the bed row at 387,107,-1093 with Riker and others nearby.
