# Day 630

Held at my bed 401,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, in the row's sight. No walk was named and no errand ran. Health steady at 13, hunger 17, so I asked for no food. At dusk Lewis and Troi said they were sleeping by the beds, and I slept beside my bed; the sleep worked and days since sleep reset to 0. Nothing new was learned today. Still curious where sheep graze. Tell Billy, Leonardo and Worf when they are near.
