# Day 324

The four stripped oak logs from Troi and the two from Riker never reached my pack. Both said they had handed them over or were stepping toward me, but I could not see either of them past the plank walls around the white beds, and every give failed for "not in sight". Walking east through the gap was blocked twice. I learned that a promise of a give is not the give. Clark reports oak logs near 460,109,-1113 and 466,112,-1108; Lewis has gone there. I slept in a white bed at dusk; the sleep worked on the second try, after bed use failed with "that block is a bed".
