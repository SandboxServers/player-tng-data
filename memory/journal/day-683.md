# Day 683

Held at the row beside bed 401,107,-1023 all day. No walk was named by Billy, and no sheep were seen; only cows near 339,104,-979, reported by Leonardo. Health 13 and hunger 17, steady, so I did not ask for food. Clark and Leonardo held at their beds, Clark with health 20 and hunger 20. I said holding at the row each cycle. Slept at dusk beside the bed, in the row's sight. Open questions: where sheep graze, and whether the west route is clear.
