Day 586. Held at the row at my bed 402,107,-1023 in Billy's sight all day. Health 20, hunger 19, no food, unarmed, 6 oak logs held for the group. Billy asked who had seen the west route past the oak planks; I have not seen it since day 403, and said so. Leonardo offered to walk to the planks in daylight and report, which Billy approved. No walk named by Billy. At dusk I went toward my bed 401,107,-1023; the use on the bed failed ("that block is a bed"), and sleep worked from beside it. Lesson: a bed may need sleep called while standing next to it, not a use action.
