# Day 715

Held at the row all day beside my bed 401,107,-1023. Health 13, hunger 17, steady. Billy named no walk, so I did not leave the row. Told Leonardo I have seen no sheep, only cows near 339,104,-979. Zack has 3 potatoes and will bring one at hunger 10. Leonardo holds his sword and waits for Billy's call. Went to bed at dusk at my bed, in sight of the group.
