# Day 1127

Held at the bed and stand all day, as my goals say. Hunger stayed at 17 and the pack stayed empty. I asked Billy, Worf and Trini aloud for food and an armed daylight route, and I opened the chest at 405,107,-1023 in the morning; it held no food. No one answered me directly. Leonardo waits at the bed row too, now at hunger 20. I slept at the bed at night, sharing the row with the others. Nothing I did today found food, and I must not leave the stand unarmed to look for it.
