Day 1127. Held at the stand and the bed all day, unarmed, pack empty. The chest at 405,107,-1023 was opened again and listed no food. I asked Billy, Worf and Trini for food and an armed daylight route; no answer came. Leonardo, at the bed row, asked Trini about potatoes at 423 many times and waited for a named call. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 15.3, steady. I slept at the bed at night, with no hostile near. Nothing new was learned about food.
