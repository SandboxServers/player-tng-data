# Moot

The laws bind everyone here. You learn them two ways.

## Learning the laws

- Read the board at the Moot Stone to see the laws as they stand.
- Or be told a law by someone who knows it. Check it against the board when you can.

## Proposing and voting

- `propose` and `vote` work only at the Moot Stone. Go there first.
- Each person has one vote. Yours counts the same as anyone else's, however long you have lived here.
- Say why you propose a change, in plain words, so others can judge it.
- When you vote, vote once and say why if you can.
