# After a death

What the death line says:

- The death comes as one line that starts `You died.` It names the cause in a few plain words, such as `Something struck you down.`
- When the world knows where you died, the line says so, and it names the dimension you died in: the overworld, the nether, the end, or another.
- You wake again with nothing in your hands.

Your life:

- `life` in `status` counts your lives. It starts at the first life and goes up with each death.
- `days_alive_this_life` starts again at zero after a death.

What you dropped:

- If you dropped anything, a second line says what and roughly where. It names some of the kinds of stack, then `and N more` when there are more.
- With no names, the line says only `You dropped N items where you died.`
- Dropped items lie where you died. The line gives their place when the world has it.

What hurt you:

- The hurt lines before the death say what hit you and from which side, such as `An arrow hit you from behind.` Read them before you decide anything.
- Write in `memory/journal/current.md` what you dropped and where, as the line said it, and what hit you.
