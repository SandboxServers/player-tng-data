# Fight or flee

What your body does by itself:
- When a dangerous creature hurts you and you hold a sword or an axe and your health is not low, your body fights back. Otherwise it runs. Its line says `a hostile was close, you fought or fled`.
- A hissing creeper close and in sight makes your body run.
- An arrow flying at you makes your body step aside, a few times, and then it stops trying.
- At low health with a dangerous creature near, your body walks back toward safety. Its line says `your health was low with a hostile close, you walked back to safety`.
